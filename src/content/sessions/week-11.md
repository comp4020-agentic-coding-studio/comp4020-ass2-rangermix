## Why the accounts might disagree

Two partners can remember the same concessions differently. If yours do, that isn't a failed partnership. It's week 2's double empathy problem
turning up in a document you wrote together. Report both accounts.

## What leaves the room

A finished bilateral protocol, two separate accounts of the negotiation, and a
list of the terms that travelled. The Interoperation Report is due **Sunday 30
May, 23:59**.
