Tuesday's rate limit, run slowly, with a partner you'll work with for three
weeks.

## Before the lab

Nothing to prepare. Partners are assigned by the tutor, deliberately mixed.
Week 11 explains why.

## The pass rule

Any card can be passed. Say "pass" and the next card comes up. No reason, no
follow-up question, no face. This is a lab rule, and it is also the first
clause every pair starts with: **either of us can decline a topic without
explaining why.**

No card in this room goes above level 2. Level 3 and 4 conversations are for
people who've put the hours in, and a lab partnership hasn't yet.

## Run sheet

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00--0:08 | Meet your partner. Level 0 only: names, how the break was, which week so far made the most sense. |
| 0:08--0:28 | **The ladder.** Take turns drawing cards: four level 0 cards, then four at level 1 (*what's something small that annoys you more than it should?*), then four at level 2 (*what's gone worse than you expected this semester?*). Both people answer each card. Either of you can pass. |
| 0:28--0:35 | Alone: note where the conversation got easier or harder, and which card you'd have passed on. This stays yours. |
| 0:35--0:55 | **Draft the shared protocol.** Three clauses the two of you can both check. Suggestions: reply time between you; how either of you says "I need a pause"; what depth your working conversations stay at. |
| 0:55--1:00 | Write down one thing you each conceded in the draft. That's the report's first data point. |

## What leaves the room

A partner, three draft clauses, and a private note. Next week's lab tests the
clauses against a week of actually working together.
