The lecture gave you the four-part field note and the asleep test. Today you
use both on real observations, and have someone check your work.

## Before the lab

Bring week 1's corridor log, and something to write on that you're willing to
hand to a partner.

You can observe from the edge of the room and exchange notes in writing.
Tell Tamsin if you would prefer the tutor to do the check.

## Run sheet

Times below run from the 14:05 start; finish at 14:55.

## A prepared exchange

Fictional practice data: in a tutorial, A begins a question while B is still
speaking. B says "one second", finishes the sentence, then says "go ahead".
A asks the question. Rewrite "B was rude" and "A never listens" using only
those events. A possible rule is: *in this group, "one second" can reserve
the current turn*. That is a hypothesis to test, not something the example
has established about all groups.

Practice data can fill the rewriting exercise. It cannot replace the real
observations and real second instance required in a submitted journal entry.

## Why a partner reads your notes

A partner may catch an impression you have written as an observation. Ask
them to point to the words that describe what happened.

Partners are assigned by the tutor to mix the room. Compare what each of you
recorded and what you inferred; keep any differences to revisit in week 11.

## What leaves the room

Checked notes, an honest record of any empty observation period, and one written-down rule. Test
the rule this week, then use the result in Sunday's journal entry: what
happened, the rule, the test, the rule now.
