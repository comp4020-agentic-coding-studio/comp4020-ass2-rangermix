This lab is different from the others. Nobody goes into a corridor, nothing
is timed out loud, and there is no pooling on the board unless people want it.
It's a quiet room with a template and an hour.

## Before the lab

Bring your phone, and your week 6 ledger if you kept one. The ledger's
most expensive rows are good candidates for your runbook's early signs.

## The room

Lights lower than usual; no background music; seats facing the walls as well
as the middle. Headphones are welcome. If you'd rather write your runbook
somewhere else entirely, tell Tamsin at the start and come back at 0:50 for
the last section, or don't. The spec is the same either way.

## Run sheet

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00--0:05 | The six runbook sections, on the screen. That's the whole briefing. |
| 0:05--0:25 | **Write the runbook.** Early signs first, while you can still remember what they felt like. Then what gets dropped, in order. |
| 0:25--0:35 | **Write the three messages.** Tutor, friends, the person you live with, or whoever your version of those is. Save them. Send one to yourself and read it as if it had just arrived. |
| 0:35--0:50 | **Plan the break.** One activity each for detachment, relaxation, mastery and control. Put nothing on the first day. |
| 0:50--1:00 | Optional: in pairs, swap one message and one "what gets dropped" item. Take anything useful. |

## Why the first day is empty

A break that starts with a list of catch-up tasks is a week 8 that started
early. The first day is where load comes off, and week 7's lecture gave the
reason: recovery research points at removing load, not at adding rest on top
of it.

## If writing the runbook is hard today

Then today is useful data. Write one line for early signs, "today, writing a
runbook was hard", and one message. Finish the rest when there's room. A
partial runbook written this week is worth more than a complete one that
never gets started.

## What leaves the room

A runbook, three saved messages, and a two-week plan whose first day is
blank. Your week 7 Field Journal entry is still due **Sunday 11 April, 23:59**.
Nothing is due during the break. The first entry after it is due **Sunday
2 May, 23:59**. All the dates are on the [journal page](/assessments/field-journal/).
