Convenes NT 101 and gives most of the lectures. Came to the subject from
protocol engineering, after a decade documenting network systems whose users
insisted they were self-explanatory, and then noticing the same insistence
everywhere else.

**Ask Wren about:** assessment, extensions, adjustments, or a problem your
tutor couldn't solve.

**Ask your tutor first about:** labs, journal feedback, and anything to do
with a single week's content. If your tutor hasn't replied within two working
days, email Wren and say so. That is the escalation path, and using it is not
rude.
