An organisation of repair handles recurring problems in speaking, hearing and understanding, and repair by the speaker strongly predominates over repair by someone else. Week 5 takes two things from it: repair is routine infrastructure, not an emergency, and the protocol gives the speaker the first chance to fix their own trouble.
