Across three studies, first impressions of autistic people formed from brief clips were less favourable, and came with less willingness to interact. When observers judged the content of what people said, without the audio-visual cues, the difference went away. The paper's own vocabulary is clinical; its finding points at the observer. The course uses it for the field note's first rule: write down what was said and done before any impression of how it came across.
