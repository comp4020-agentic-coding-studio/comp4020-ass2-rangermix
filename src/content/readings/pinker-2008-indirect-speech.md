A hint lets a listener decline without anyone acknowledging a refusal. Language also negotiates what kind of relationship two people have, and saying something outright can renegotiate it. And a direct statement becomes common knowledge in a way a hint doesn't, even when both people know exactly what was meant. This is the strategic reason behind week 3's envelope.
