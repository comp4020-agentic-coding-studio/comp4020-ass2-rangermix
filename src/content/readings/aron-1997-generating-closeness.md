The procedure behind the widely shared question lists: self-disclosure tasks that “gradually escalate in intensity” produced more closeness than small talk. Making closeness an explicit goal, or matching people on attitudes, made no significant difference. Week 9 takes the gradient, not the speed: the procedure works in steps both people take together, and a lab session is not an invitation to escalate that fast anywhere else.
