Keltner, Gruenfeld and Anderson propose that elevated power activates approach, with more disinhibited behaviour, while reduced power activates inhibition, with more attention to threat and to other people's interests. The account has been contested in print. Week 12 uses it for the course's closing observation: whoever holds less power runs more of the protocol, and pays the ledger's costs more often.
