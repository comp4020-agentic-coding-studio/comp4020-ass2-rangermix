Two studies tested six apology components, alone and in combination. Apologies with more components were rated more effective, some components mattered more than others, and apologies for competence failures landed better than apologies for integrity failures. The course checked the abstract, which doesn't rank the components, so week 5's apology template lists its parts without claiming which matters most.
