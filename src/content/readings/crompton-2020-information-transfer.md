Crompton and colleagues ran diffusion chains (autistic, non-autistic and mixed) and measured how much of a story survived each retelling. Mixed chains lost detail significantly faster and reported lower rapport; autistic and non-autistic chains did not differ significantly. It is the experiment behind week 11's title: the loss happens between implementations, not inside one of them.
