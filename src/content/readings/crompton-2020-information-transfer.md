Crompton and colleagues ran diffusion chains (autistic, non-autistic and mixed) and measured how much of a story survived each retelling. Mixed chains lost detail significantly faster and reported lower rapport; autistic and non-autistic chains did not differ significantly. Week 11 uses that comparison to challenge an account of autistic people as simply unable to share information. The course checked the abstract's comparison between chain conditions, not a link-by-link explanation of the additional loss. Its proposal to negotiate a bilateral protocol remains a course interpretation to try, not an intervention this study tested.
