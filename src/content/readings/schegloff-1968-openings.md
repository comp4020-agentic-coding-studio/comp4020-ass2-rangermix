Schegloff set out to “ascertain rules” for how conversations open, and named properties of those rules, among them *conditional relevance*. The course uses the paper for one fact before any of its content: the opening handshake was specified, in a peer-reviewed journal, in 1968. Week 1 treats that as the course's founding scandal. Only the abstract was checked, so the course names the term and leaves its definition to the paper.
