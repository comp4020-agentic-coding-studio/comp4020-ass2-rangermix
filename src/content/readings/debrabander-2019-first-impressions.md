Autistic and non-autistic raters made similar trait judgements from short videos of autistic adults. But only for non-autistic raters did those judgements reduce interest in interacting. The course keeps this reading because it complicates the neat version of week 11: both implementations perceive the difference; what varies is whether the difference counts against someone.
