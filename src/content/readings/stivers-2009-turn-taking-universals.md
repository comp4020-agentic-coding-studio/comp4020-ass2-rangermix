Across ten languages, conversations avoided overlapping talk and kept gaps short, and average gaps stayed within 250 ms of the cross-language mean. The authors suggest people are sensitive to small tempo differences and experience them as large differences in style. Read it for how precise the timing is, and for why a slightly different tempo can feel like a different personality.
