Coding the diffusion-chain videos for mutual gaze and backchannelling showed that the markers didn't map onto rapport the same way across pairings. Less backchannelling went with higher rapport between autistic people. The visible signs of engagement are implementation-specific, so part of interoperating is agreeing which signs count.
