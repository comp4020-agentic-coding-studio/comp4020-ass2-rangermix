Twenty-three autistic adults co-produced a definition: exhaustion, withdrawal, difficulty with executive function, reduced functioning, and more pronounced autistic traits, distinct from depression and from ordinary burnout. Two independent methods arriving at nearly the same description is why week 7 treats the definition as settled enough to plan against.
