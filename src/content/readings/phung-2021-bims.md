Burnout, inertia, meltdown and shutdown — BIMS — have been identified as important parts of some autistic people's lives. This study builds on the authors' earlier academic descriptions of those phenomena, which came from autistic adults, and extends them through one-to-one interviews with **eight autistic children and youth**, using a flexible interview approach that allowed augmentative communication and visual supports. Their reflexive inductive thematic analysis found BIMS described as a multi-faceted experience with emotional, cognitive and physical components, and one that is often misunderstood by the people around them.

Week 7's planning model separates overload, shutdown and burnout. This paper discusses **shutdown** and also names **inertia** and **meltdown**, which the model omits. The sample is eight children and youth; this annotation makes no claim about adults or prevalence. The course's three time scales are an incomplete map.
