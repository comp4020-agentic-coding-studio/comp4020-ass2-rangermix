In 195 responses from telephone calls, most acceptances and rejections came at similar speeds. But once a transition reached 700 ms or more, rejections clearly outnumbered acceptances, and even small departures from a normal gap made a plain “yes” less likely. Listeners have learned the pattern, which is why a slow answer is heard as a reluctant one. Week 4 calls that inference the undocumented timeout.
