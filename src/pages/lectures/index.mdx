# Lectures

Tuesday {lectureSchedule.start} to {lectureSchedule.end}, every teaching week, in Theatre 1.
Break {lectureSchedule.breakStart}–{lectureSchedule.breakEnd}; the core material
ends around {lectureSchedule.coreEnd}. Remaining time is contingency or optional
discussion of the deck's closing question. Each lecture writes
down one part of the protocol, cites where its claims come from, and ends with
lines you can check yourself against. The lab that Thursday tests it. Dates,
rooms and the rules the room runs on are on the [timetable](/timetable/); each
