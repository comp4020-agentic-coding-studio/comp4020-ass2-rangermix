From over five thousand adults, the authors identified five styles of communicating romantic interest: traditional, physical, sincere, playful and polite. They are self-reported, so the inventory measures how people think they signal, not what receivers understand. Week 12's open issue on romance starts here: if there are five dialects, misreading one isn't a personal failing.
