The greetings literature kept asserting things about greetings without agreeing what one is, so Duranti proposes six criteria for identifying a greeting exchange across languages and speech communities. Applied to a speech community in Western Samoa, the criteria pick out four types of greeting exchange — and those exchanges contradict a standard claim, because they are not devoid of propositional content and need not be “expressive” acts in the speech-act sense. Samoans use greetings to do social work, including searching for new information and sanctioning behaviour. Week 1 uses this twice: as the model for specifying anything precisely enough to argue about, and as the reason the course calls “the reply is a token, not an answer” a property of this protocol rather than of greeting as such.
