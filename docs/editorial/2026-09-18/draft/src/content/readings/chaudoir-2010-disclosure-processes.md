Chaudoir and Fisher's model covers any identity that can be kept hidden. The discloser's goals, whether approaching something or avoiding something, change what disclosure does, and its effects run through relief from concealment, social support and changes in what others know. Week 10 builds partial disclosure on the third route: you choose which information changes.
