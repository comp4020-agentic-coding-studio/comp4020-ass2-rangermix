Studies of other people's views, mostly using vignettes, found disclosure improved acceptance. Studies of autistic adolescents and adults found reluctance and fear of stigma. The review also notes the field's own assumption that disclosure should happen. Week 10 teaches this disagreement as the central fact, rather than picking a side.
