Cialdini and Goldstein organise the compliance and conformity research around three goals people pursue: seeing things accurately, keeping relationships, and feeling good about themselves. Much influence works subtly and outside awareness. Week 12 sketches persuasion as the protocol used on purpose, and this is where the reading starts.
