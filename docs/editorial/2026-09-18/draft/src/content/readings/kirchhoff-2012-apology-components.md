Two experimental vignette studies in Germany. Study 1 found that including more verbal elements in an apology increased forgiveness, especially after a more severe offence, and that the relationship between an apology's completeness and forgiveness was *mediated by anger reduction*. Study 2 found that for a more severe offence four elements were particularly relevant: conveying emotions, admitting fault, a statement of apology such as “I apologize,” and an attempt at explanation.
